%% ── Job Market Paper ──────────────────────────────────────
\section{Job Market Paper}

\textbf{``Avoiding the British: Brexit, Cross-Border Syndication, and the
Identification of Mechanisms under Market-Wide Shocks''}
\\[0.4em]
Did Brexit break the co-investment ties between British and continental European
venture capitalists? I summarize my findings. First, continental VCs with high
pre-determined exposure to UK partners durably cut the share of their syndicates
including a British investor by 8.2 percentage points against a base rate of 18.7\%, and
among thirteen partner-country corridors subjected to an identical, pre-specified
protocol the United Kingdom is the only one to move. Second, established UK--continental
dyads were no more likely to dissolve than comparable continental dyads (triple
difference $+0.3$ pp, $p=0.77$). Third, the aggregate volume of jointly syndicated deals
tracked control corridors within $[-20\%, +26\%]$: the relationship thinned at the
new-syndicate margin without existing ties rupturing or deal flow collapsing. To arrive
at these conclusions, I set up an exposure-based event study on more than 1.2 million
Crunchbase investor--round observations, control for small$\times$post composition, and
conduct inference by wild-cluster bootstrap with few clusters, running the same
falsification protocol across every partner-country corridor. The methodological lesson
comes last: when a market-wide shock treats every agent on the same date, designs
without an untreated unit do not lose precision --- they manufacture mechanisms.

%% ── Working Papers ────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Working Papers}

\textbf{``Pooling Contracts between Venture Capital Institutions''}
(with Pierre Fleckinger)
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
\item An informed-principal framework for contracts between institutions in venture
capital. Under ex-post commitment --- the accelerator-to-VC relationship --- the unique
optimal contract is pooling with a binding budget constraint on the agency, and some
positive-NPV projects are necessarily excluded. Under ex-ante commitment --- the
LP-to-VC relationship --- the budget constraint is slack and all profitable projects are
financed in cash-rich economies. \emph{JEL:} G24, G23, D82, D86.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.3em}
\textbf{``Bridge Financing under Accelerator Contracts:
A Model of Y~Combinator's 2022 MFN SAFE Reform''}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=1pt, topsep=2pt]
\item A sequential financing game (accelerator, bridge investor, seed investor,
entrepreneur) analysing the most-favored-nation clause in accelerator contracts. The
equilibrium bridge share is independent of the amount bridged; the linear MFN is the
unique detail-free schedule implementing the efficient cap-table overhang for every
financing need; and the gain from a richer instrument is quadratic in the ratio of
bridge needs to overhang value --- nil on the empirically relevant domain.
\end{itemize}
